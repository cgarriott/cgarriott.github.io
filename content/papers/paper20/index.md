##### Abstract

Repos involving at least one non-U.S. counterparty make up a large share of the U.S. repo market, with the greatest concentration in non-centrally cleared bilateral repo (NCCBR). Using OFR's new NCCBR data, the authors find that cross-border cash flows within NCCBR are split almost evenly between flowing out and flowing in. Banks, dealers and hedge funds are the main participants: hedge funds mostly borrow cash, while banks and dealers often trade with their affiliates. Most cross-border repo is in U.S. dollars and backed by U.S. collateral. Cross-border repo broadens the investor base for U.S. securities and supports liquidity, but it can also transmit shocks across jurisdictions.
