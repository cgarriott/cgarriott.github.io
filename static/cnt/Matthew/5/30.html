:

καὶ εἰ ἡ δεξιά σου χεὶρ σκανδαλίζει σε, ἔκκοψον αὐτὴν καὶ βάλε ἀπὸ σοῦ, συμφέρει γάρ σοι ἵνα ἀπόληται ἓν τῶν μελῶν σου καὶ μὴ ὅλον τὸ σῶμά σου εἰς γέενναν ἀπέλθῃ

And if your right hand tripsa you up, cut it off and cast it from you, for it is profitable for you that one of your members should perish, and not that your whole body should departb into gehennac.

Notes

a
skandalizei The noun names the trigger-stick of a trap — the part that springs it. From there it comes to mean anything that brings another down. English reaches for "stumbling block," "offend," or "cause to sin," none of which keeps the image of a trap set for someone, and "scandalize" has drifted away from it entirely.↑
Usually offended, cause to offend, fall away
b
apelthēi ↑
c
geennan Gehenna is a place name, not a description: the valley south of Jerusalem associated with child sacrifice in the prophets and later with judgement. English "hell" replaces the place with a developed doctrine, and merges it with two other words — Hades and Tartarus — that the Greek keeps separate.↑
Usually hell, gehenna
